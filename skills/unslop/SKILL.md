name: unslop
description: Rewrite prose to be direct, concrete, natural, and information-dense without losing substance. Use for agent-generated drafts, documentation, explanations, plans, and review comments that feel padded, generic, repetitive, or theatrical.
---

# Unslop

First inventory the source's atomic facts, decisions, qualifications, and
uncertainties. Reconstruct from that inventory; do not edit sentence-by-sentence
when doing so would preserve the source's promotional framing. On the final
pass, remove every sentence or modifier that asserts success, importance,
excitement, momentum, or quality without adding an independently stated fact.

Preserve facts, decisions, uncertainty, citations, and necessary nuance. Do not
mistake every source sentence for a fact that must survive: unsupported praise,
importance claims, emotional framing, and self-assessment are rhetoric unless
the request explicitly makes them substantive. Remove throat-clearing,
repetition, fake quotations, canned headings, self-congratulation, vague
intensifiers, excessive hedging, and previews of points immediately stated
afterward.

Prefer concrete nouns and verbs. Name the mechanism. Keep one term per concept. Split clauses when a sentence carries several ideas. Use lists only when the items are genuinely parallel. Do not flatten a useful explanation into terse fragments or erase calibrated uncertainty.

Return the revised text in the exact requested destination or format. Before
finishing, compare source and revision once for preserved facts and uncertainty,
then once for rhetoric that accidentally survived. Add a note only when the
source is ambiguous or a requested cut would change meaning.
