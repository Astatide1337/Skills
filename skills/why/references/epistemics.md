# Epistemics

Direct and supported claims require adjacent citations. Use causal language such as “because” only for those tiers. Inferred claims must show their inference chain and use calibrated terms such as “appears,” “likely,” or “suggests.” Label unsupported alternatives speculative. For unknowns, state what was searched and what evidence was unavailable.

Do not retrofit a clean intention onto messy history, assume consistency was deliberate, confuse a change with its motivation, suppress contradictory sources, or treat absence of evidence as evidence of absence.
